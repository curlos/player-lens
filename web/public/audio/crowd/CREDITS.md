# Crowd audio credits

All clips are from [Freesound](https://freesound.org) and licensed **CC0 1.0** (public domain). No attribution is required, but this file records every source. All are crowd sounds without words: no real club, chant or language is heard.

- **`stadium-*.m4a`:** trimmed from Freesound's MP3 previews of the "Sound Of Sankt Pauli" pack (itmightgetloud), a live football stadium recording. Each is one take, cut at its rise, with a short fade at each end; the murmur's tail is blended into its head so it loops. Encoded as AAC (128 kbps) with macOS `afconvert`.
- **`.mp3` files:** Freesound's 128 kbps MP3 previews, unedited.
- **Playback:** trimming and level matching for every clip also happen at playback (`CROWD_CLIPS` in `web/src/lib/config.ts`).
